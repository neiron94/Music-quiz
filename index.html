Music Quiz

Welcome to Music Quiz!

Think you know your music? Put your knowledge to the test with Music Quiz — a fun and interactive way to challenge yourself and discover new artists and genres!

  • Customize your quiz by selecting a genre or musician, adjusting the number of questions, and choosing how long each song clip plays.
  • Play instantly with a randomly generated set of music questions.
  • Explore music genres and artists to learn more about them and start themed quizzes with a single click.

Each question plays a short clip — your goal is to guess the correct answer from four options. Ready to play?
